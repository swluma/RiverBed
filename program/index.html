Tile Legend
Fountain (Red/Blue)
Owner gets a flat bonus when used; opponent using it shares 50% of FINAL word points to the owner.
Gold (Winner’s Footsteps)
If a found word uses ≥1 gold tile: add a flat bonus per gold tile used. Spawned for the active player’s turn only.
White (Failure into Opportunity)
When you fail to find a word, the tiles from that failed swipe turn white first, then random non-white tiles fill the rest (up to the level’s max). On your next turn, if a found word uses ≥2 white tiles, multiply its score; the tiles disappear afterward. Spawned for the active player’s turn only.
Gray (Counter bonus)
Gray tiles cannot be used for words. They muddy/desaturate the entire tile.
Purple (Value Decay)
Opponent-only: purple tiles from your last word appear only after they scored, and they clear the moment they miss. Each word touching purple loses extra decay step % (points go to you) and the tiles disappear at turn end.
Spell Finder hint
Green highlights show the first letters of your Spell Finder word (only visible to you).
Multiple states
A tile can show multiple colored segments (fountain+gold+white, etc.). Gray overrides usability.
Skill Reference
How to Play (Quick & Cheery)
Welcome to Rogue Boggle Duel! Each turn is a quick word-hunt sprint, and every skill is a spicy twist. Ready? Let’s go!
1) Swipe a word
Press and drag across adjacent tiles to spell a word. Release to lock it in, then press Confirm. Longer words = bigger base points.
2) Chain your combo
Successes stack a combo multiplier. A miss resets it, so play boldly but cleanly!
3) Watch special tiles
Gold gives a bonus, White boosts big when used twice, and Fountains share points. Gray tiles are off-limits.
4) Grow your skills
Pick upgrades when offered. Mix categories to unlock extra bonuses and mind games.
5) First to the target wins
The match ends when someone reaches the Win Score. Change it anytime from the top bar.
6) Want help?
Use “Hint” to reveal a path by trading 3 skill levels. It’s a comeback button, not a freebie.
Tip: open “Legend” and “Scoring” anytime if you want the numbers. Now go find that glorious word!
